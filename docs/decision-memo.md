**Cost sensitivity.** Holding the all-month 60% BIL / 40% VT reference mix fixed, 25 bps of additional annual cost reduces its estimate to 6.36%, below the 6.5% screen. Reoptimizing at 25 bps shifts it to 55% BIL / 45% VT. At 100 bps the all-month mix moves to 45% BIL / 55% VT and estimated volatility rises from 6.05% to 8.27%. All four illustrative scenarios find a feasible mix in both windows, but the largest difference between windows stays above the five-point review condition.
